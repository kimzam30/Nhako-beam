Flip5 cover screen

A small view for the outer screen with only what you need while the phone is closed: say yes or no to a request, watch it come in, and open it when it lands. Drawn at true size from the device's own numbers. Turn on Spec overlay to see the safe area.

Measured layout

Screen374 × 360 dp, landscape (748 × 720 px at 320 dpi), read with adb from the Flip
Camera cutoutBottom right, 184.5 × 33 dp (px 379–748 × 654–720). The system reports a 33 dp bottom inset.
Safe area16 dp on the top and sides, 33 + 16 = 49 dp at the bottom: 342 × 295 dp for content
ButtonsTwo of (342 − 12) / 2 = 165 × 48 dp, bottom edge at 311 dp, 16 dp clear of the cutout
Bolt112 dp, the same 3D model and moods as the main screen
CornersDrawn at 40 dp. The real corner radius isn't reported over adb, so the 16 dp margin is there to cover it.
Not hereSettings, history, picking files and codes stay on the main screen. Tapping anything else says "Open the phone to continue" (new copy, yours to change).
