Pages and overlays

Empty states, onboarding, and the toasts, banners and sheets, in the Flip5 main screen at true size (1 px here = 1 dp, 360 × 880). The text is the app's own copy. The icons use style A until you pick one on the icon page. The bolt is the real 3D model.

Empty states

Today they show a card or a sleeping bolt. I give each one a mood that says what is going on: still searching, nothing yet, or no Wi-Fi.

The same empty home on the tablet and desktop

Content keeps the 560 max width from the tokens and centres. Tab S9 FE measured at 823 × 1317 dp (1440 × 2304 px, 280 dpi). Desktop window default 1080 × 760, minimum 360 × 560.

Onboarding

Five steps, as today. Step dots stretch from 6 to 22 dp on the snappy spring. Steps slide 24 dp on the gentle spring. Nearby and Notify are Android only.

Toasts, banners and sheets

The banner drops 8 dp and fades in 220 ms. The toast rises 24 dp on the settle spring (430 ms, 1.5% overshoot) and drains its 2 dp timer over 4 s. The sheet slides up on the smooth spring (430 ms) over a 220 ms scrim. They all leave on the exit ease in 150 ms.
