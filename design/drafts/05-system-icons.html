Launcher, notification and tray icons

These are the app's real files, read from the repo, on the new #2F8FFF. Every size below is measured against the platform's own rules. Turn on Spec overlay to see the safe zones.

Android launcher (adaptive icon)

The launcher crops the 108 dp canvas to 72 dp in whatever shape the phone uses. Everything that matters has to sit inside the 66 dp safe circle. The faint layer shows what is cropped away.

Your call: keep the bleed, fit the square, or fit the circle?

Themed icon (Android 13 and up)

When the owner turns on themed icons, the launcher uses the one-color drawable and tints it from the wallpaper. The colors here are typical examples. The shape is the real file.

Notification icon

The status bar icon is white only, 24 dp, with a 20 dp live area. On the Flip5 (480 dpi, 3x) it is 72 × 72 real pixels.

Desktop tray, taskbar and app list

The pixel bolt is drawn on a 48 grid. It only stays crisp at 48 px and whole multiples of it (96, 144, 192). At 16, 22, 24 and 32 px the art falls between pixels and the face turns to mush, which is what Windows and Linux panels show today. Below the pixel art, I propose a flat bolt for 32 px and under, using the same shape the app animates (Bolt.svelte), yellow with the navy offset on #2F8FFF.
