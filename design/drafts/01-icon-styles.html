The rules every icon follows

Grid24 × 24, live area 20 × 20 (2 px padding). Round shapes may reach 1 px further to look the same size as square ones.
Stroke (A, C)16 px → 1.5 px, 20 px → 1.75 px, 24 px → 2 px. Stroke is set per size so lines stay crisp, not scaled.
CornersRound caps and joins, 1 px corner radius on boxes, 2 px on device outlines.
Navy offset (B)Exactly 1 device px down and right, from 20 px up. At 16 px it is dropped because it would blur into a smudge.
Active stateA and C switch to their solid shape (B without the offset) when selected, like Fill in the app today.
ColorOne color, from the text or accent token. Contrast is checked against canvas, card and accent at 3 : 1 or more (WCAG 1.4.11).
PlacementIcons sit on whole pixels at 1x, 2x and 3x. Hit areas stay 48 dp on Android, 32 px with a mouse.
Your call: A, B or C? A mix is possible too, for example B for the main buttons and A everywhere else.
